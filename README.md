# 🚀 Featured Projects

## ✈️ Airline Operations Intelligence & Performance Analytics

Analyzed **1.85M+ flight records across 9 major airlines** to identify operational bottlenecks, delay patterns, airport performance, and weather-related disruptions.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, SQL, Power BI, Statistics

### 🔍 Key Work

- 📊 Analyzed **1.85M+ flight records** across multiple airlines.
- ⏱️ Evaluated on-time performance and flight delay patterns.
- 🌦️ Combined flight information with historical weather data to study weather-related disruptions.
- 🛫 Identified airports, routes, and time periods associated with higher delay frequency.
- 🧹 Performed data cleaning, validation, transformation, and exploratory analysis using Python.
- 📈 Built interactive Power BI dashboards for operational performance monitoring.

### 📌 Key Metrics

- **1.85M+** flight records
- **9** airlines analyzed
- **75.5%** overall on-time performance
- **~30%** delay rate during adverse weather

## 💳 UPI Transaction & Fraud Risk Analytics

Analyzed **20K UPI transactions** to identify fraudulent transaction patterns and understand how fraud risk varies across transaction amount, merchant category, transaction type, city tier, and time-related factors.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, SQL, Power BI, Statistics

### 🔍 Key Work

- 💳 Analyzed **20K financial transactions**.
- 🚨 Identified **763 fraudulent transactions**.
- 📊 Calculated and analyzed a **3.82% fraud rate**.
- 💰 Evaluated approximately **17.54M transaction value**.
- 🏪 Analyzed fraud behavior across merchant categories.
- 🌆 Compared transaction risk across city tiers.
- ⏰ Investigated transaction timing and fraud patterns.
- 📈 Developed Power BI dashboards for fraud monitoring and risk analysis.

### 📌 Key Metrics

- **20K** transactions
- **763** fraudulent transactions
- **3.82%** fraud rate
- **17.54M** transaction value analyzed

## 📦 Supply Chain & Sales Operations Intelligence

Analyzed approximately **791K orders** to evaluate sales performance, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, returns, and supply chain operations.

**Tech Stack:** Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, K-Means, Prophet

### 🔍 Key Work

- 📦 Analyzed approximately **791K orders**.
- 🚚 Evaluated delivery performance and operational efficiency.
- 🔄 Investigated the relationship between delivery delays and product returns.
- 👥 Used **K-Means clustering** to segment customers into:
  - High-Value
  - Regular
  - Occasional
  - Low-Value
- 📈 Developed a **90-day revenue forecast using Prophet**.
- 📊 Built Power BI dashboards to monitor sales and operational KPIs.
- 🎯 Converted analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

### 📌 Key Metrics

- **791K** orders analyzed
- **35.1%** delivery delay rate
- **90-day** revenue forecast
- **4** customer segments
